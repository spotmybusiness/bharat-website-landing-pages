## 1. Specification: Moving Checklist & Pre-Move Preparation Guide

### 1.1 Page Overview
- **Proposed URL**: `/moving-checklist`
- **Page Title**: `The Ultimate Home Shifting Checklist & Pre-Move Guide — Bharat Relocators`
- **Meta Description**: `Step-by-step moving checklist and timeline guide for home shifting in Kolkata & PAN India. Learn how to prepare, pack essentials, and avoid moving day mistakes.`
- **Content Type**: Pillar Guide / Educational Resource (Type C)
- **Priority**: **HIGH**

### 1.2 Purpose & Intended Audience
- **Purpose**: Provide prospective movers with an actionable, structured timeline (4 weeks prior, 2 weeks prior, 3 days prior, Moving Day, Post-Move) to organize their relocation smoothly.
- **Intended Audience**: Families, working professionals, and individuals planning a local Kolkata or intercity household move.
- **Search Intent**: Informational & Commercial Pre-Purchase (`home shifting checklist Kolkata`, `how to prepare for house moving`, `packers and movers preparation guide`).

### 1.3 Required Page Sections
1. **Hero Section**: Title, concise overview, estimated reading time, and direct link to `/get-a-quote`.
2. **Interactive / Printable Moving Timeline**:
   - *4 Weeks Before Move*: Decluttering, inventory sorting, booking survey with Bharat Relocators.
   - *2 Weeks Before Move*: Address updates, utility transfers, packing non-essentials.
   - *3–5 Days Before Move*: Refrigerator defrosting, essential bag preparation (medicines, documents, electronics).
   - *Moving Day Protocol*: Crew supervision, inventory verification, meter reading, final walk-through.
   - *New Home Arrival*: Unpacking sequence, furniture assembly inspection, room placement.
3. **Hazardous & Prohibited Goods Notice**: Clear table of items movers cannot legally or safely transport (gas cylinders, paint, inflammables, loose valuables).
4. **Room-by-Room Packing Tips**: Practical advice for kitchen glassware, wardrobe clothes, and delicate electronics.
5. **Mid-Content & Bottom Conversion Units**: Embedded quote CTA, coordinator phone click-to-call, and WhatsApp button.

### 1.4 Related Existing Pages & Internal Links

- **Inbound Linking Sources**: Linked from `/household-shifting` (preparation section), `/faqs`, and footer resources.

### 1.5 Required Evidence & Client Confirmation
- **Client Confirmation Needed**: None for standard moving advice. Prohibited items list adheres to standard road transport safety regulations.

## 2. Specification: Vehicle Transportation RTO Documentation & Safety Guide

### 2.1 Page Overview
- **Proposed URL**: `/vehicle-transportation-guide`
- **Page Title**: `Vehicle Transportation & RTO Documentation Guide — Bharat Relocators`
- **Meta Description**: `Complete guide to two-wheeler and car shipping from Kolkata. Understand mandatory RTO paperwork, pre-transit vehicle preparation, and enclosed carrier safety.`
- **Content Type**: Pillar Guide / Regulatory Resource (Type C)
- **Priority**: **HIGH**

### 2.2 Purpose & Intended Audience
- **Purpose**: Demystify the legal, regulatory, and mechanical requirements for shipping motor vehicles across state borders in India.
- **Intended Audience**: Bike and car owners relocating out of Kolkata for jobs, education, or personal reasons who need complete clarity on vehicle safety and RTO documentation.
- **Search Intent**: Informational & High-Trust Commercial Investigation (`documents required for bike transport from Kolkata`, `car carrier rules India RTO`, `how to ship bike safely Kolkata`).

### 2.3 Required Page Sections
1. **Hero Section**: Clear positioning on vehicle transit safety, RTO compliance, and quick quote CTA.
2. **Mandatory Documentation Checklist**:
   - Registration Certificate (RC Book / Smart Card copy).
   - Valid Comprehensive Motor Vehicle Insurance.
   - Pollution Under Control (PUC) Certificate.
   - Vehicle Owner Government ID Proof (Aadhaar / Voter ID / Passport).
   - Bank NOC (if vehicle is under active hypothecation and moving permanently).
3. **Pre-Transit Mechanical Preparation Checklist**:
   - Fuel tank draining (leave strictly < 25% or reserve level to prevent fire hazard).
   - Removal of mirrors, GPS mounts, and detachable accessories.
   - Deep cleaning and photography of existing body scratches/dents.
   - Battery disconnection guidance for long-haul car shipping.
4. **Carrier Protection Comparison**:
   - Enclosed / Closed Container Truck (dust, weather, stone-chip protection) vs. Open Carrier.
   - Custom Wooden Crating for high-end motorbikes vs. multi-layer bubble wrapping.
   - Hydraulic ramp loading mechanics.
5. **Joint Pre-Move Condition Report & Delivery Inspection**: How the condition sheet protects the customer during transit insurance claims.
6. **Conversion CTAs**: Dedicated buttons to `/car-shifting`, `/bike-shifting`, and `/get-a-quote`.

### 2.4 Related Existing Pages & Internal Links
- **Parent/Contextual Links**: Links to `/bike-shifting`, `/car-shifting`, `/track-your-shipment`, `/get-a-quote`.
- **Inbound Linking Sources**: Linked from `/bike-shifting`, `/car-shifting`, and `/faqs`.

### 2.5 Required Evidence & Client Confirmation
- **Client Confirmation Needed**: Verify exact fuel reserve policy (e.g. max 2–3 liters or empty) and whether Bharat Relocators assists with state RTO NOC agent referrals if requested.
